This FIR concerns a multi-state investment fraud that has come to light after a written complaint from Arvind Kulkarni, director of a textile firm in Surat. According to him, three persons, namely Tarlok Bansal urf Lala, Deepa Nair and Farhan Sheikh s/o Iqbal Sheikh, promised him assured returns on a commodities scheme and collected ₹2,50,00,000 from him in two instalments. The money was received in Kotak account 6412345678, which is controlled by Bansal. From there, INR 1,75,00,000 was forwarded to PNB account 3987001234567890 controlled by Nair. Bansal used mobile +91 98211 44556 to speak with Sheikh on 83690 11223, the handset of which carries IMEI 867123045678901. The complainant says the total loss is about 2.5 crore. Registered on 25.10.2026 as FIR 301/2026 u/s 318(4), 61(2) BNS. Dy.SP Nitin Desai has taken up the probe.
